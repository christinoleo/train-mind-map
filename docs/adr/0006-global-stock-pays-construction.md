# Construction draws from a global stock; the player never carries items

Everything in the Core and in Boxes adds up to one global stock, and placing anything deducts from it automatically. Items fly from the nearest eligible Box to the build site instantly (with animation). A Factorio-style player inventory, with dragging items in and out, was rejected as too fiddly on touch screens. Hand crafting also does not exist: taps yield only raw resources.
