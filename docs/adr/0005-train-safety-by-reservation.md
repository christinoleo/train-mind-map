# Trains avoid collisions by automatic segment reservation, not player-placed signals

Rails are always double track. Before entering a segment or crossing, a train reserves the path to the next point where it can stop, so collisions are impossible by construction. Factorio-style block and chain signals were rejected because they are the most complex part of Factorio trains and awkward to place on a phone. The puzzle moves to layout instead: crossings, interchanges, depots and capacity. Train logic (T1–T5) is edited through form-based rules, not circuit wiring.
