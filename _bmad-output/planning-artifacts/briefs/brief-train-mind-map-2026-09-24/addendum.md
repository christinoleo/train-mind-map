# Addendum — train-mind-map brief

## Market & comparables research (2026-09-24, web research digest)

### Direct competitors (node graph as the factory)
- **Node Factory** (Steam, Sep 2026, desktop, idle): nodes wired into pipelines on an abstract canvas, heat and power limits. No map, no crossing rule. Closest on theme.
- **Nodes** (Steam, 2022, 56% of 41 reviews positive): a node-graph factory with a tech tree. Weak reception suggests a bare node graph is not enough on its own.
- **Factory Planner: First Sparks** (Steam, 2025, 81% positive): card nodes wired at right angles, capacity and speed limits. No spatial map.
- **Industry Idle** (browser, mobile, Steam): buildings on a map with implicit links, where distance costs transport. Proves that a map-based factory works in a mobile browser.
- **Gap:** no existing title combines a spatial resource map, edge throughput, a no-crossing rule and trains.

### Inspirations
- **Factorio:** reference for trains and throughput. Desktop only.
- **shapez / shapez 2:** clean, abstract factory. shapez 1 on mobile costs $6.99, with 7 free levels.
- **Mindustry:** about 9.3M Google Play downloads.
- **Builderment:** over 1.5M mobile downloads. A relaxing factory game that works on touch screens.
- **Station to Station:** rail-built supply chains with discounts for track and bridges. 89% positive. The closest match on feel.
- **Rail Route:** minimalist dispatching.
- **Railbound:** short rail puzzles. A model for onboarding.
- **Dorfromantik and Zachtronics games:** calm tile placement and optimisation leaderboards.

### "Lines cannot cross" precedents
- **Planarity:** the pure untangling puzzle.
- **Mini Metro:** tunnels and bridges are a scarce weekly resource.
- **Mini Motorways:** upgrade choices after each week. A bridge counts once however long it is. Motorways skip traffic.
- **Freeways:** overpasses are the whole puzzle.
- **Pattern:** planar by default, with bridges and tunnels as scarce resources the player earns.

### Train and batch-transport patterns
- **Mindustry mass driver:** batches of 10 to 120 items, fixed range, fires every 3.33 s, needs power.
- **shapez 2 trains:** a stop plus one loader or unloader per wagon, with filler wagons for spacing.
- **Factorio:** schedules with wait conditions (full, empty, time). Signals are the main source of complexity; avoid them.
- **Mini Metro:** a line is a colour through stations; carriages add capacity.
- **Formula:** throughput = (load ÷ trip time) × number of trains.

### Market notes
- Premium puzzle and transport games do well on mobile: Mini Metro has sold up to about 6M copies across platforms.
- Deep factory games work on touch screens when the controls are simple (Mindustry, Builderment).
- Browser portals favour idle and incremental factory games. Evidence that deep automation games succeed in the browser is thin (uncertain).
- **Risk:** pure node-graph factory games have been niche or poorly received.
